ABOUT THIS MODEL

Ideal two-body motion around a fixed Sun, with orbital shapes and orientations held constant in J2000 axes. Planet–planet interactions and precession are omitted. Earth’s path uses the Earth–Moon barycenter as an approximation to Earth, and its force uses Earth’s mass. Halley uses JPL’s sample 1P/Halley elements (epoch MJD 49400), with q = 0.58597811 AU and e = 0.96714291. Its idealized period is 75.31 years; actual returns vary. The schematic tail points away from the Sun and is not a force vector. This is a teaching model, not a live ephemeris.

Orbital distances share a true linear scale; body sizes are schematic. The camera rotates the view without changing the physics. NASA/JPL sources: orbital elements, Table 1 ↗ and planetary masses ↗; Halley’s orbital elements ↗ and NASA’s Halley overview ↗.
