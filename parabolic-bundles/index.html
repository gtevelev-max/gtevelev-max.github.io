What am I seeing?+

The points represent the eigenvalues of quantum multiplication by the first Chern class, normalized as λ/q, for the moduli space with n = 2g + 1 marked points. The parameter ρ runs along the positive real interval.

At ρ = 1, the terminal values are 4(−1)k(2g − 1 − 2k), for k = 0,…,g − 1. A marker may represent several eigenvalues; the cluster cards give the total multiplicities. With Multiplicity enabled, marker radius grows logarithmically with multiplicity, so large and small clusters remain visible. Marker area is not proportional to multiplicity.

Fit final sets the horizontal span so that the interval between the outermost terminal values occupies exactly 90% of the plotting width. Zoom, pan, and Fit all let you inspect branches outside that interval. Both complex-plane axes use the same unit scale.

In Cluster evolution, the horizontal axis is ρ and the vertical axis is the real part of λ/q. Conjugate branches can overlap in this projection; use the complex plane to see their imaginary parts.

Computation & geometric walls+

Branches are computed from the spectral equations and continued numerically. Animation interpolates between sampled parameter values; the terminal spectrum is exact.

The point ρ = 0 is a singular limit of the full family. The demonstration does not assign a finite full spectrum to it. Near-zero previews use the smallest positive sampled parameter, reported below the animation.

The optional wall guide uses val = −log and a reference value qref = e−b. It marks ρ = exp(−(n − 4d − 2γ)b/d), corresponding to the weight wall a = 2d/(n − 2γ), where d ≥ 1, γ ≥ 0, and 2d + γ ≤ g. Changing b changes the displayed locations. They are not canonical singularities of the complex coefficient ρ. The terminal chamber is lightly shaded in the evolution view; the plotted algebra is always that of the terminal moduli space, so eigenvalues are not removed at a wall.

The separate Spectral crossings option marks the two verified coincidences of distinct spectral branches for g = 3, at approximately ρ = 0.07522257 and ρ = 0.97476747. These are coincidences of eigenvalues, not assertions that the corresponding critical points degenerate. Crossings for higher genera are not tabulated here.
