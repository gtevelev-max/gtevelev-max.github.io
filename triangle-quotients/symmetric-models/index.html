What “maximally symmetric” means here

We seek the largest group of rotations of ordinary three-dimensional space that realizes symmetries of the marked surface, without self-intersections. The Riemann surfaces have more automorphisms than can be displayed as rigid rotations. Their intrinsic metric still comes from the triangle-group gluing, not from the lengths and angles of these drawings.

The Klein model retains all 336 original chambers. The Wiman and Fricke–Macbeath models have the matching topology and rotational actions; transferring their 720 and 1,008 original chambers to these shapes remains unfinished. Their meshes are deliberately shown without a substitute chamber pattern. The existing gluing presentation has not been replaced by these previews.
