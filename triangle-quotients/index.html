Why these diagrams define Riemann surfaces

Exact data and sources

The generators are permutations. Enumeration gives the group order. Every edge has two incident chambers, every vertex link is one circle, and the two chamber colors give a consistent orientation. These checks determine a closed connected orientable surface. Its conformal structure comes from gluing the geometric triangles, not from the distortion of the drawing.
